ADVANCE RIDE REQUEST

Weekend Fare Policy

Fares for Saturday and Sunday pickups may be higher depending on demand and availability. All pickup times are in Central Time. Before you book or pay, RYDORA will provide a quote showing any weekend adjustment and the total price.

Once you pay, RYDORA will honor your quoted fare even if demand changes or another passenger cancels. If you request a change to your route, pickup time, passenger count, or services, RYDORA will give you a revised quote for approval before collecting any additional payment.

For shared airport rides, RYDORA confirms the departure once riders have paid for the advertised number of seats. If RYDORA cannot operate the departure, you will receive a full refund.

Child Passenger & Car Seat Policy

All child passengers must be properly restrained in accordance with applicable law in each state where the ride operates, including Tennessee’s child passenger restraint law (Tenn. Code Ann. § 55-9-602) and, when applicable, Kentucky’s child restraint law (KRS § 189.125). As a condition of service, RYDORA requires an appropriate, properly installed child safety seat or booster for each child passenger, including infants. The restraint must be suitable for the child’s age, height, and weight and used in accordance with the restraint and vehicle manufacturers’ instructions. Each child counts toward the three-passenger limit. Car seats and luggage may reduce available capacity.

Disclose each child’s age, height, and weight when booking, and state whether you will bring an appropriate restraint or request one from RYDORA. You may bring a suitable restraint at no additional charge. A RYDORA-provided restraint costs $35 per seat and must be requested in advance and confirmed before payment. Availability is limited. Request it through the custom ride form so the fee can be included in the quote before payment.

RYDORA will cancel the ride if any child does not have a suitable, properly installed restraint at pickup. If you agreed to provide the restraint and it is missing or unsuitable, the $7 cancellation fee applies and the remaining prepaid balance will be refunded. If RYDORA cannot provide a suitable restraint it previously confirmed and the ride must be canceled, you will receive a full refund.

Advance-booked rides are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, subject to availability. All times are Central. Blocked dates are unavailable. Choose a pickup on the hour or half hour.

Every child must have an appropriate car seat or booster. RYDORA will cancel the ride if the required restraint is unavailable at pickup. Request a RYDORA-provided seat in advance for $35 per seat, subject to confirmation.

Scheduling & Multiple Stops

Request rides at least 24 hours ahead. Changes and additional stops require at least two hours’ notice and confirmation. Shorter-notice requests depend on availability. Pickups are spaced at least 30 minutes apart, with additional time allowed for travel. Full advance payment and booking confirmation are required.

Each fare covers one pickup and one destination. Additional stops and planned waiting require a revised quote and confirmation in advance.

Five-Minute Wait & Missed Pickups

The driver waits five minutes from the scheduled pickup time, or from arrival if the driver arrives late. If the rider is not ready, the ride is canceled and a $7 cancellation fee applies. For a prepaid missed pickup, RYDORA deducts the $7 fee and refunds the remaining balance paid for that ride.

Rider Cancellations

If you cancel at least two hours before pickup, RYDORA refunds the full amount prepaid for that ride. If you cancel less than two hours before pickup, RYDORA deducts a $7 cancellation fee and refunds the remaining balance.

Driver Cancellations & Refunds

If your driver cancels your ride, RYDORA refunds the full amount you paid for that ride, with no cancellation fee. The missed-pickup policy above applies when the driver arrives and waits five minutes for you.

Approved refunds are initiated within two business days; bank posting times depend on the payment provider.

Passengers, Luggage & Safety

Reservations allow up to three passengers, subject to seat belts, child restraints, and luggage space. Disclose all passengers when requesting your quote. Riders must disclose children during booking and follow the Child Passenger & Car Seat Policy in this agreement. Luggage must fit safely. Pets require advance approval. Service animals are accommodated without a pet surcharge or certification requirement.

Can I schedule a ride in advance?

Yes. Rydora rides are reserved in advance. Choose a future pickup date and time. Pickups are spaced at least 30 minutes apart, with additional time allowed for travel.

How much will my ride cost?

Local rides start at $16 total per one-way trip after discounts. Your solo fare depends on the pickup, distance and driving time. Local shared rides qualify for the group discount when everyone travels the same route together. Each return pickup is priced separately. Request a quote before paying.

Which areas do you serve?

We serve Clarksville and quote surrounding-area trips individually. Advance-booked rides are available 24 hours a day, seven days a week, subject to availability. All times are Central. Blocked dates are unavailable.

Does this form book a real ride?

No. This form prepares an email request for you to send. Rydora reviews availability, route details, and your requested pickups before confirming the reservation. Call (844) 931-7433 or email rydora.rides@proton.me for booking support.
